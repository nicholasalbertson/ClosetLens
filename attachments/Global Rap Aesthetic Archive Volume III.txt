Global Rap Aesthetic Archive · Volumes I–III
Compiled 2026-10-26. Research and original outfit-design archive.
Coverage: 25 artist profiles; 37 sourced fashion-identification references, 3 era/style reconstructions, 50 original head-to-toe looks, 8 vest concepts, 6 face-covering concepts and 10 jewelry/grill briefs. Total: 90 outfits/reference looks.
Reliability key: D = documented apparel as listed by an editorial archive or photographed source (not authentication of ownership); R = photo-era reconstruction or unverified item breakdown; C = original fashion concept invented for this collection. Unidentified garments stay explicitly unverified. Prices omitted because listings change. A vest labeled fashion has no ballistic performance claim.

A purely cosmetic removable overshell must not compromise the underlying certified assembly. Never label a fashion vest bulletproof.
