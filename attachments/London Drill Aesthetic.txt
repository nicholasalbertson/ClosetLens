11. Custom tactical-fashion vest design library

The red, distressed Glo Gang-style vest in your uploaded references is an excellent example of the visual direction: bold typography, paint splatter, layered panels and aggressive graphic composition.

These are original armor-inspired fashion designs, not protective body armor. If genuine ballistic protection is desired, use a complete independently compliant product, with its protection verified against NIJ Standard 0101.07⁠￼. Cosmetic customization must not compromise certified protective components.

12. Grillz, face coverings and iced-out jewelry

Diamond-style grillz

Cuban chains and pendants

Oversized links, graduated layers, custom initials, elaborate pendant bails

Designer face coverings

Rib-knit balaclavas, tactical-inspired hoods, sport neck gaiters, graphic fabric masks

Wrist and finger stacks

Stone-set watches, tennis bracelets, oversized signets, pavé bands and studs

For the custom jewelry library, I’ve specified design categories rather than simply listing jewelry brands: 8–14 mm Cuban-style chains, 4–6 mm tennis necklaces, 45–70 mm personalized pendant faces, diamond-style studs, matching signets, luxury watches and removable dental jewelry.

For grillz specifically, use a professional dental impression and fitted removable piece. The design should respect the wearer’s bite, tooth condition and material sensitivities.
